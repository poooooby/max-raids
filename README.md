# Max Raids

Sword / Shield's Max Raid Battles on the Gen 3 games of gen1recomp (Emerald, FireRed, LeafGreen,
Ruby, Sapphire). A giant Gigantamax boss takes on four of your party in a 3D den: shields, Max and
G-Max moves, ten turns and four faints, and a catch at the end.

**Requires [National Dex Gen 3](https://github.com/poooooby/national_dex_gen3)** (the bosses' and
your Pokémon's modern moves, the Fairy type and abilities). G9 Battle Sprites (Gen 3) is
recommended for your Pokémon's animated back sprites.

Work in progress: a raid opens and plays its intro; dens, the battle and the rewards are coming.

## Options

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| BATTLE PAD | MAIN SCREEN (the command menu over the den) or SECOND SCREEN (sent to the device's second display; the main screen keeps the whole den) |
